La durata in Shots vale per tutti gli shot del Roll. Lanciali a gruppi, cambiando la durata prima di ogni gruppo.

| Gruppo | Durata | Shot | Crediti |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 4 s | 1, 3, 5, 6 | 7 ciascuno |
| B | 8 s | 2 | 12 |

Costo, 720p: 7+12+7+10+7+7 = **50 crediti**. I frame costano 0. È il tetto: un rifacimento sfora la giornata. Il primo piano del papà non è uno shot a parte: è il first frame del pull-back, così il viaggio resta un gesto solo.

Ogni shot usa first e last di quella scena. Non mettere il frame della scena prima, tranne dove sotto è indicato apposta perché l'azione continua nello stesso posto.

### Shot 1 — Caricano, senza Zyppy

Durata 4 s. Technique: nessuna. First: F1. Last: F1.

```
The family of four strains to load too many bags into the open trunk of the dark grey SUV. The father lifts a duffel and wipes sweat, the mother shoves a suitcase in, the children sag against the pile. The camera starts on the bags at the bottom of the vertical frame and tilts up to their tired faces. They do not speak. Warm late-afternoon light, polished 3D animation. Only effort, zippers and bags, no music.
```

### Shot 2 — Arriva Zyppy e dice la battuta

Durata 8 s. Technique: nessuna. First: F1. Last: F2.

```
The family is still struggling with the bags. Zyppy, the blue suitcase mascot, steps up into the lower half of the vertical frame, sees them, and stops them with one raised gloved hand. He points at the luggage and speaks one clear line in Italian: "Perché viaggiare così pesanti? Zyppy pensa a ritirare i vostri bagagli e a portarli a destinazione per voi!" The family freezes and looks down at him. The camera tilts down to him as he arrives, then holds. Warm late-afternoon light, polished 3D animation. Only Zyppy's voice.
```

### Shot 3 — Prenotano

Durata 4 s. Technique: nessuna. First: F2. Last: F3.

```
The father pulls a phone from his pocket and holds it up in the lower center of the frame. He and the mother lean in, scroll the Zyppy page, and tap once to book the luggage pickup. Their tired faces lift into a smile, with a soft sigh and a small gasp of delight. The children look up and smile. Zyppy watches, silent. Slow push-in toward the phone. Warm late-afternoon light, polished 3D animation. Only the parents' sighs and wonder sounds, no words, no music.
```
